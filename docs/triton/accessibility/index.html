Accessibility.

Triton's accessibility settings make the whole of Triton easier to use: calmer animations, a quicker way across several screens, and switches for features you may not want. They apply to all of Triton, not to one app.

Each setting has its own page, listed under Accessibility in the sidebar.

  1. The settings
  2. Opening Accessibility

The settings

Settings → Accessibility has four sections, one for each setting:

SettingWhat it does
Anti Motion SicknessCalms Triton's animations, one switch per animated feature, plus Enable all. Off by default, under Anti motion sickness.
Edge BumperTap the outer edge of your screens to jump the cursor to the other side, or hold it there for quick options. On by default, under Navigation & pointer.
Gesture ControlAllows hand gestures through the camera. When it is off, nothing can start gesture control. On by default, under Gesture control.
Clipboard TileShows the ClipBoard tile, with your recent clipboard history, in the bottom bar's quick panel. On by default, under Quick panel.

Every switch takes effect as soon as you change it. You never need to restart Triton.

Opening Accessibility

  1. Open Settings.
  2. Choose Accessibility.

You can also search Settings. Words such as motion, dizzy, bumper, teleport, gesture, camera or clipboard find the right section. Searching a11y finds the Accessibility page itself.

The status line

The top of the Accessibility page shows a short status line, for example:

MOTION · CALM   BUMPER · ON   GESTURES · OFF
PartMeans
MOTION · CALMEvery Anti Motion Sickness option is on.
MOTION · PART CALMSome of them are on.
MOTION · FULLNone are on: Triton plays all its animations.
BUMPER · ON / OFFWhether the Edge Bumper is on.
GESTURES · ON / OFFWhether gesture control is allowed.

Where the settings are saved

Triton saves most of these settings in its own settings file, settings.properties. The file lives with Triton, not in the Windows registry, so a portable copy of Triton keeps your choices when you move it to another machine.

SettingSaved in
Anti Motion Sicknesssettings.properties, for every user of this Triton
Edge Bumpersettings.properties
Gesture Controlgesture.properties, next to the gesture models
Clipboard TileThis computer's Java preferences, so it does not travel with Triton
