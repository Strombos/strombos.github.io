Clipboard Tile.

The ClipBoard tile in the bottom bar's quick panel pops up your recent clipboard history, so you can get back something you copied earlier. If you don't want it there, hide it.

Find it in Settings → Accessibility → Quick panel. It starts on.

The quick panel and the tile

Click the tray in the bottom bar to open the quick panel. It has a tile for each quick tool:

TileOpens
ClipBoardYour recent clipboard history.
VolumeThe volume controls.
BrightnessThe brightness controls.
NotificationsThe notification centre.
KeyboardThe on-screen keyboard.

The ClipBoard tile is the first one, and the only one you can hide from Accessibility.

Hiding or showing the tile

  1. Open Settings and choose Accessibility.
  2. Under Quick panel, untick or tick Clipboard tile.

The tile disappears from the quick panel, or comes back, straight away.

Where it is saved

Unlike the other Accessibility settings, this one is saved in this computer's Java preferences, not in Triton's own settings file. On Windows that is the registry. So it does not travel with a portable copy of Triton: on another machine the tile starts on again.
